# Technology and AI

This domain examines how technologies shape attention, relationships, agency, work, and meaning—not only what the tools can do.

Questions for this domain:

- Which capabilities genuinely support living well?
- Which defaults or incentives capture attention and distort priorities?
- Where should interaction be redesigned rather than managed through willpower?
- How can technology remain a tool without becoming the author of the user's goals?
